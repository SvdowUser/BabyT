How it works

BabyT combines meme culture, character evolution and collectible plush concepts. The website checks the live market cap and updates which mystery plush stages are unlocked.

1. Grow

The community grows the project and helps bring more attention to BabyT.

2. Unlock

Each milestone unlocks a new mystery plush stage on the website.

3. Reveal

Unlocked stages can later be revealed as concept designs and giveaway ideas.
