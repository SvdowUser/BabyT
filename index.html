Brainrot Games

Brainrot Games is built around BabyT, the characters and the community behind the project. Brainrot Battles is the first game and brings that world to life in a way people can actually play.

$BabyT is the community token of the project. Over time, we want to give it more use across the ecosystem, for example through selected in game items, cosmetics and community perks.
The token will not be required to play the game.

WHAT IS BRAINROT GAMES?

Brainrot Games started with Brainrot Battles, a multichain MMO where players explore new areas, collect Brainrots and bring them into battle. The game is already playable in the browser, with more characters, regions and games planned.
