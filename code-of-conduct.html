Community rules

Keep it good.

Respect people

Do not harass, threaten, target or demean other community members. Disagreement is fine; personal abuse is not.

No scams or impersonation

Do not impersonate the project, staff, moderators or other users. Do not post fake contract addresses, phishing links, fraudulent giveaways or misleading support messages.

No spam

Avoid repetitive promotion, flooding, unrelated advertising and deliberate disruption of community channels.

Keep content appropriate

Do not post illegal content, malicious files, doxxing, graphic harassment or material intended to harm others.

Moderation

Community spaces may remove content or restrict accounts that repeatedly or seriously break these rules. Enforcement may vary by platform and its own terms.
