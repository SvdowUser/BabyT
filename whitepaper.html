Overview

The project.

BabyT

BabyT began as a character and community project and is expanding into a browser game, physical merch and an on-chain token ecosystem. The project is built around the character first; the token is one part of it, not the entire product.

Brainrot Battles

Brainrot Battles is the playable game connected to BabyT. A public browser alpha is already available while additional areas, systems and gameplay are still in development.

Merch

A physical BabyT plush sample exists. Final manufacturing, pricing and availability have not been announced. No preorder is offered through this site unless explicitly stated on the merch page.

$BabyT

The original BabyT token is on Solana. A wrapped BabyT token also exists on BNB Chain. Always verify contract addresses on the official token page before interacting with any token, pool or third-party exchange.

Direction

The current focus is to grow the project through an actual playable product, continued character content and selective physical releases. Features and plans may change as development progresses.

Risk notice

BabyT is a meme token and crypto assets are highly risky. Nothing in this paper is a promise of profit, price performance, guaranteed utility or future value.
